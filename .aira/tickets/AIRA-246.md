## Triage 2026-10-12 (goal: clear remaining known issues)

**Disposition:** retired. **State on master v0.34:** still-open.

Speculative hygiene with no measured problem. The slowdown symptom is bounded by AIRA-242 plus AIRA-280 paging, and the remaining bloat (about one short row per one-off argv) is plausibly tens of MB over years; the ticket cites no numbers. A cap adds a retention policy (what horizon, and whether pruning old signatures should drop learned reserves and shift the machine-wide ConfinePeakP90 prior used by admission, confine_peak_history.go:205), plus a per-insert DELETE on the daemon write path. That is new machinery for an unmeasured cost, which cuts against the repo's simplicity rules (keep the primitive and document the gap). Close it, or hold it with a reopen trigger of a measured state.db size or dump duration. Do not hand-pick a horizon without the owner.

**Evidence:** Unfixed: internal/store/confine_peak_history.go:157-160 only trims per (kind,signature) to confinePeakHistoryLimit=20 (line 13); no total-row cap or age prune anywhere (grep of DELETE FROM in internal/store shows none other for this table). Signature = full effective argv joined with NUL (internal/runner/resource_estimate.go:82-95), so distinct signatures do grow without bound. But both stated symptoms are already mitigated: AIRA-242 gave dump/budget a 30s dumpHistoryTimeout (commit 37e823d, internal/daemon/admit.go:41-63, pinned by confine_history_deadline_test.go), and AIRA-280 (commit b634dd8, v0.32) added opt-in streamed paging, 4 MiB pages, one row at a time, bounded daemon memory (internal/daemon/confine_history_page.go; store.StreamResourceBudgetSubjects at confine_peak_history.go ~line 318). Hot admission read is a per-(kind,signature) indexed lookup (line 185; index at store.go:879/1320), unaffected by table size. The ticket file has no body or comments, status planned, no milestone, no relations, not held. No measurement of actual table size or dump time exists in the repo.

## Measurement 2026-10-25 (from AIRA-314; status unchanged)

The figures the retirement above said were missing, measured on a fresh copy of the live `state.db` with `docs/dev/aira-314-p90-index-measurement-repro.sh` (sqlite3 CLI, WSL2, exit 0): 185,738 rows, 124,316 signatures, 102,903 single-row subjects, 8,656 signatures qualifying for the machine-wide p90 (3+ usable peaks).

- The diagnostic reads this ticket was about are not the hot cost. The cost that matters is on the ADMISSION path: `store.ConfinePeakP90` (the machine-wide p90 prior) is a full-table `GROUP BY` over every confine row. Plan `SEARCH ... USING INDEX confine_peak_history_kind_signature (kind=?)` plus a temp B-tree; 0.209 / 0.209 / 0.204 s, against the 250 ms admit deadline it shares with the per-signature read (`admitHistoryTimeout`). Earlier runs the same day: 0.15-0.32 s. Reported by the AIRA-314 plan review, measured in Go through the real store on a copy of the live DB: under a 250 ms context the read failed 10 of 10 times; a failed read is not cached, so each admission falls back to the 1 GiB unpinned default.
- The per-signature history read stays ~1 ms (indexed), as this retirement said.
- A covering `(kind, signature, peak_rss)` index brings the p90 read to 0.038 / 0.061 / 0.091 s (plan `USING COVERING INDEX`), one-time build 0.503 s on this copy. Not built: whether to make the p90 rung reliable or delete it is the owner's AIRA-317(2) question; AIRA-314 spec Appendix A holds the design.
- No row cap was built and none is proposed: the admission cost is answered by an index (if the rung is kept) or by deleting the rung, not by pruning learned history.
