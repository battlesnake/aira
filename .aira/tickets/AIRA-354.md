Owner 2026-11-19: 'something needs to change': system RAM 40/79 GB while jobs queued at a ~43 GiB ceiling. Measured: 43.35 GiB booked vs 17 GiB real anon (26 jobs, 10 queued); at the 2026-11-18 13:20 incident bookings were 41.5 GiB vs ~22 GiB SUM OF PEAKS (about 1.9x); AIRA-324 enforce trial at 0.9 (10 percent discount on pinned jobs only) cut the queue 13-23 to 0-11 with host MemAvailable min about 35 GiB (floor 8). The ledger check is sum(bookings) <= ceiling (checkedAvailable, admit.go), a worst case in which every job uses its whole booking at once. Design: allow bookings above the ceiling by a measured factor / observed-peak charging, keep the live-RAM floor and per-scope memory.max as the safeguards. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-measured-overcommit-design.md.
