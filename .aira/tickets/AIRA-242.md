## Note 2026-10-25 (from AIRA-314; status unchanged)

"Admission unaffected (per-signature read is small)" missed one admission read. The per-signature read is indeed ~1 ms, but admission also reads the machine-wide p90 prior (`store.ConfinePeakP90`, through `cachedAdmitPeakP90`), a full-table `GROUP BY` over every confine row under the same 250 ms admit context. Measured on a fresh copy of the live `state.db` (185,738 rows, 124,316 signatures; `docs/dev/aira-314-p90-index-measurement-repro.sh`, sqlite3 CLI): 0.204-0.209 s; earlier runs 0.15-0.32 s. Reported by the AIRA-314 plan review in Go through the real store: 10 of 10 reads failed under 250 ms, and a failure is not cached, so the prior is in practice off on this box and unpinned cold starts get the 1 GiB default. Whether to fix the read (covering index, keep-last-good cache) or delete the p90 rung is held for the owner as AIRA-317(2); the design is AIRA-314 spec Appendix A.
