As built (v0.27): with no card reading (sampler never armed, or nvidia-smi unreadable) the System VRAM panel draws the reserved jobs against the CONFIGURED budget (AIRA_VRAM_BUDGET), labelled "budget"/"unreserved", with a note naming the honesty state; the daemon now publishes the configured budget in every state. If no budget is configured (auto-detect) and the card is unread there is no width to draw, so the panel stays UNEVALUATED with a Reason that names the ledger total. Honest scope: a --vram job cannot be admitted without a fresh reading, so this helps (a) an idle box (empty budget bar) and (b) nvidia-smi failing while a job holds VRAM; it is not "reservations without a reading" in general.
