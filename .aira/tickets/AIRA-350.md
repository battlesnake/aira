Owner decision 2026-11-18 (supersedes the earlier dynamic lane, which could not prove a head cannot starve): "go back to a fixed slice that is separate to the main slice reservation. Its max size is always fixed. The main slice reduces its admission ceiling based on available RAM as it currently already does." Earlier wording: "a fixed slice of x GB (4 or 8), which jobs requesting <= y GB (1 GB) are admitted to before using the main slice; if full they use the main slice; default mode only, not CI mode."

STATUS (spec round 3, 2026-11-18): BLOCKED on the owner's answer to Q1; not ready for the plan gate. The non-convergence tripwire fired (six review rounds across both designs without approval; round 2's family-A fix produced two new P1s in that family).

Design (spec round 3): a separately-booked pool of fixed size x inside the one queue and ledger (AIRA-228; not a second cgroup). No pool is stored on a lease: every pass derives "small" leases (scoped, parentless, reserve <= y, no live sub-reservation) from the fields the ARDR frame carries, so a restart is neutral. The lane holds min(S, x) of their footprint (reserve + 64 MiB each) and main is charged max(0, S - x). A job-level waiter whose ledger charge is <= y (scoped, signed, not a suite parent, sub-reservation or exclusive) is tried in the lane first while S + its footprint <= x, past the head-of-line freeze and bypass budget, leaving the head's CPU and VRAM; closed behind a pushed head. Main waiters get min(W, P), where P is main's ledger room and reads no lane state while S <= x; a main grant is checked on everything it adds to main, including a suite parent's move out of the lane when its first worker is granted. The fork (owner, Q1): a fixed lane, today's host bound and a head-safe main pool cannot all hold while the pressure ceiling falls (proof in spec §2.0). Rows: A, the lane on top of today's ceiling and always open (the owner's words; provisional default; worst-case free memory 7.6 GiB at 26 jobs, inside the watchdog band; realistic low about 26 GiB); B, out of main's ceiling and shut when the ceiling falls (round 2; big jobs lose 2.8 GiB on average); C, out of main's ceiling and always open; D, half and half. Every row keeps total bookings within the slice's memory.max (per-scope OOM containment). Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-fixed-small-lane-design.md.
