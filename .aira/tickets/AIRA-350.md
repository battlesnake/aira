Design (spec round 4): one ledger, one ceiling, every reader unchanged (sum of all bookings + headroom <= ceiling at every grant, so no new host exposure). A grant-only pre-pass runs before today's evaluator walk: each eligible waiter (booking, i.e. ledgerCharge, <= y; job-level, scoped, not exclusive, signed and not a --delegate-ram suite parent), in today's evaluation order, is granted on today's RAM/physical/CPU/VRAM fit alone, exempt from the head rule, the AIRA-307 budget and the fairness hold, while S + its booking <= x, where S is the summed booking of all granted job-level leases booking <= y (derived by a walk, never stored or incremented; restart-neutral up to leases whose booking rises to its pin). Guard G: a lane grant past the reference waiter (the first queued job-level waiter that could fit an empty slice, derived each pass) is withheld unless that waiter would fit a slice holding only the small leases and this one, in RAM on pins, CPU and VRAM (admitCouldFitEmptyLocked generalised with an occupancy argument). Bounded guarantee: the reference is granted once every lease the lane did not grant while it was the reference has ended (ceiling not fallen); a head may wait for up to min(x, E - H - its reserve) more bookings to end than today. Closed behind a pushed reference (AIRA-309). Lane full or kept back: the small waiter is an ordinary main waiter (today's rules) and is retried in the lane every pass.
