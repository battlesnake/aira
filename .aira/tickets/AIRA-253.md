Deferred during the AIRA-252 aira board Increment-2 review (Fable). The shared no-TTY TUI coordinator (runTUIRuntime / coordinateShutdown / appRunDone, AIRA-134) handles a Stop → retry cycle, but if the cancellation lands BEFORE app.Run() starts the screen, the retry path can be left with a dead/uninitialised screen rather than either re-initialising cleanly or exiting. Not triggered by the board's normal open/close or mode-switch paths (those are covered and green); this is a robustness edge in the coordinator's Stop-retry handling. Scope: make the coordinator's retry-after-cancel-before-Run either re-init the screen or exit deterministically, with a regression test that reds against the current path. Read-only board feature otherwise complete and shipped in v0.10.
