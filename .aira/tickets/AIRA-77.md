Found during the whole-project simplification review (PR #12), adjacent to the AIRA-67/PR#7 admission work. If a project loads both the legacy xdist-governor plugin and aitest, `--delegate-ram` arms both simultaneously — the xdist governor's own per-test reservation mechanism and aitest's per-worker cgroup caps both become active on the same job, rather than exactly one of them. Not investigated further — needs tracing to confirm the actual double-booking behavior and whether it's just wasted/redundant reservation or a genuine correctness risk (e.g. double-charging the admission ledger for the same physical memory). Relevant to any project (like fastest-ee) mid-transition from xdist to aitest, where both plugins may legitimately be present during the migration window.

Also noted in the same review: 80% of `confine_peak_history` signatures are singletons (5,103 of 10,253 rows) — the per-signature RAM estimator almost always falls through to the machine-wide prior in practice. Not a bug, but worth knowing when reasoning about how much value the per-signature estimation machinery is actually providing versus its complexity cost — relevant context for anyone picking up PR #7's admission-simplification proposals.
